# Spacing

Tokens from `ui/styles/theme/spacing.css`. The spacing rhythm is even steps plus 1 — `1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, …` — and the root is 12px below `xxxl` (1600px) and 14px from `xxxl` on, so one step is 3px or 3.5px and `p-2` is 6px or 7px. The px values on this page are at the 12px root; conversions read a source px value at the 14px root. Convert a px value to the nearest even step (ties round down) rather than writing `[Npx]`; a sub-step hairline (a border, a divider, a ruler tick) converts to the fixed `px` step instead of rounding.
